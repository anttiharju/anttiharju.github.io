# URLs

- [Zig Makes Rust Cross-compilation Just Work](https://actually.fyi/posts/zig-makes-rust-cross-compilation-just-work/). Well, not really, there's various edge-cases that prevent you from cross-compiling certain crates, but pretty nifty. I cross-compile macOS binaries from Linux CI hosts with a Nix-based version of this in my [`compare-changes`](https://github.com/anttiharju/compare-changes) project.
- [Per-project Nix substituters](https://blog.ielliott.io/per-project-nix-substituters). I think Nix gets _a lot_ of the fundamentals **right** (not that it's always intuitive or even user-friendly coming from the not-Nix world) and while AI is rather useful for getting a desired config out, I really struggled in understanding how the caches and trusting them on a machine works before this blog post.
- [The Workman Keyboard Layout Philosophy](https://workmanlayout.org). I don't actually use it. But I think it helped me establish the right mental model in terms of that some fingers are stronger than others, and I should strive to put the repetetive actions on those fingers. I am a fan of the ZSA Voyager and make heavy use of it, and my layout for it can be found on [Oryx](https://configure.zsa.io/voyager/layouts/jvM3o/latest/0). Although I've also customized the firmware a bit, find my customisations on GitHub: https://github.com/anttiharju/zsa-voyager.
